Reel Editor

  • Freelance
  • Remote
  • Per reel
Future role

Turn our printer time-lapses, packing videos and customer stories into short reels people actually watch.

What you'll do

  • Edit 15–30 second reels for Instagram and YouTube Shorts
  • Add captions, Hinglish text and trending audio
  • Deliver 2–4 reels a week from our raw clips

You'll fit if

  • You've edited reels before (CapCut, Premiere Pro, VN or similar)
  • You know what makes people stop scrolling
  • Bonus: you love gadgets or making things

Social Media Manager

  • Part-time
  • Remote or Ahmedabad
Future role

Run our Instagram and LinkedIn: plan posts, write captions, reply to DMs and grow the MakeMeProto community.

What you'll do

  • Plan a weekly posting calendar and write captions
  • Reply to comments and DMs, and pass orders to us
  • Track what works and try new ideas every month

You'll fit if

  • You write well in Hinglish and English
  • You've managed a page or brand account before
  • You're organised and reply fast

3D Design Intern

  • Internship
  • Ahmedabad or Remote
  • 3–6 months
Future role

Learn real product design: turn customer photos and sketches into print-ready 3D models, with an engineer reviewing your work.

What you'll do

  • Model parts in Fusion 360, Tinkercad or SolidWorks
  • Measure, design and test fits for replacement parts
  • Help prepare files for printing

You'll fit if

  • You're a student or fresher in mechanical, design or similar
  • You've made at least a few 3D models (college projects count)
  • You're curious and enjoy fixing things
