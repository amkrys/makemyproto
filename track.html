Idea se darwaaze tak.

Every order moves through the same six steps. We update your status as soon as it moves.

  1. 🧾Received

    We have your request and files. We check them and send you a final price on WhatsApp.

  2. ✅Confirmed

    You've approved the price and paid the advance. Your order joins the print queue.

  3. 🖨️Printing

    Your part is on the printer. Big or multi-part orders can take a few runs.

  4. 📦Ready

    Supports removed, checked against your size and finish, and packed with care.

  5. 🚚Shipped

    Handed to the courier, or ready for pickup in Ahmedabad. Tracking details appear here.

  6. 🎉Delivered

    It's with you. Tell us how it turned out; we love seeing prints in their new home.

How long does it take? Most small prints are ready in 2–4 days after your advance. Design projects and large quantities take longer; we give you a date with the quote.
