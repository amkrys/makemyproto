Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. The short version

  • We only collect what we need to quote, make and deliver your order, reply to you, or consider a job application.
  • Your STL file stays in your browser when you use the instant price calculator. It is not uploaded to us.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers on this website.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: contact@makemeproto.com.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email and WhatsApp number when you message us or fill a form.
  • Order details: what you want made, files, photos, sketches and measurements you send, and your chosen material, colour and quantity.
  • Delivery details: your address for local delivery or courier.
  • Payment records: the amount, date and UPI or bank transaction reference. We don't see or store your card details, UPI PIN or bank passwords.
  • Job applications: the details, portfolio link and resume you choose to send through our Careers page.
  • Website settings: your language choice (Hinglish or English), stored only in your own browser.

3. How our forms work

Our quote, contact, 3D Adda and job application forms don't send data to a server. When you press send, they open WhatsApp on your device with a pre-filled message. Nothing is shared until you press send in WhatsApp yourself.

WhatsApp is run by Meta. Messages you send to us there are also covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

4. How we use it

  • to prepare quotes, design, print and deliver your order;
  • to reply to your questions and send updates (for example a photo while your part prints);
  • to keep records for accounts and tax;
  • to review job applications and contact applicants;
  • to improve our service. We'll only send offers or news if you ask for them, and you can stop them any time.

5. Who we share it with

We only share what's needed, with:

  • Courier and delivery partners (name, phone, address) to deliver your order;
  • Payment providers and banks to process payments;
  • WhatsApp, email and hosting providers we use to talk to you and run this website;
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data, and we never share your designs with anyone without your permission.

6. How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: as long as Indian accounting and tax laws require (usually up to 8 years).
  • Your design files: for up to 2 years after your order so we can reprint parts for you, or less if you ask us to delete them.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • General messages: deleted when no longer needed.

7. Keeping it safe

Your data is stored on password-protected accounts and devices that only the two of us use, with two-step verification where available. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you promptly if a breach affects your data.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it (unless we must keep it by law, for example invoices);
  • withdraw consent you've given, for example to marketing messages;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

Email contact@makemeproto.com or WhatsApp +91 84870 72210. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves your language choice in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address when loading them.

If we add website analytics in the future, we'll update this page first.

10. Children

Our services are meant for adults. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order or contact us for you.

11. Contact and grievance officer

For any privacy question or complaint, contact our grievance officer: Bal Krishan Swami, MakeMeProto, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India · contact@makemeproto.com · +91 84870 72210.
