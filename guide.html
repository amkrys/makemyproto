Designing it yourself? Ye padh lo.

A few rules that save time, money and failed prints. If this sounds like too much, just send the file and we'll check it for you.

I have my STL ready →

Deepak ki diary ✏️ print-ready STL ke 6 rules

  1. Walls ≥ 1.2 mm. Patli deewar = toota part.
  2. Overhangs under 45° print without supports. Zyada jhuka? Supports lagenge.
  3. Moving / fitting parts: 0.2–0.3 mm ka gap chhodo.
  4. Max 25 × 25 × 25 cm in one piece. Bada hai? Hum tod ke jod denge.
  5. Export in millimetres. Warna 2 cm ka part 2 metre ka ban jaata hai 😅
  6. Text at least 1 mm raised or deep, so it actually shows.

— D.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Yes. Send a photo, sketch or measurements, or the broken part itself. We create the 3D model for you (design starts from ₹300 per hour, depending on complexity).

How much does 3D printing cost?

Printing starts at ₹5 per gram for PLA. Price depends on weight, material, infill and quantity. Use the instant quote above for an estimate. Minimum order is ₹300.

How long does it take?

Most small prints are ready in 2–4 days. Design projects and large quantities take longer, and we'll tell you the timeline with the quote.

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, 3MF, OBJ and STEP. If you have something else, send it and we'll check.

What is the maximum size you can print?

Up to about 25 × 25 × 25 cm in one piece. Bigger objects can be printed in parts and joined.

How do I pay?

UPI, bank transfer or cash. 50% advance to start, and the rest on delivery.

Do you deliver?

Yes, local pickup or delivery in Gurugram and Delhi NCR, and courier across India.
