Designing it yourself? Ye padh lo.

A few rules that save time, money and failed prints. If this sounds like too much, just send the file and we'll check it for you.

Meri STL ready hai

Deepak ki diary ✏️ Print-ready STL ke 6 rules

  1. Walls ≥ 1.2 mm. Patli deewar = toota part.
  2. Overhangs under 45° print without supports. Zyada jhuka? Supports lagenge.
  3. Moving / fitting parts: 0.2–0.3 mm ka gap chhodo.
  4. Max 25 × 25 × 25 cm in one piece. Bada hai? Hum tod ke jod denge.
  5. Export in millimetres. Warna 2 cm ka part 2 metre ka ban jaata hai 😅
  6. Text at least 1 mm raised or deep, so it actually shows.

— D.

🧾 Ordering

How do I place an order?

Send us what you have on WhatsApp: an STL file, a photo, a sketch or just a description. We reply with a clear price and timeline. When you say yes and pay the 50% advance, we start. You can also get an instant estimate first on the Instant price page.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Yes, most of our customers don't have one. Send a photo (with a ruler or coin next to it for scale), a sketch, measurements or the broken part itself. We create the 3D model for you and show it to you before printing.

Design work starts from ₹300 per hour. Simple parts like a clip or knob usually take under an hour; we always tell you the design cost before starting.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, ₹300 per order. That covers machine setup, checking your file and finishing. Small items like a single keychain cost ₹300, and you can often get several small items within that same ₹300.

Can you print large quantities?

Yes. We regularly quote for 10 to a few hundred pieces, like branded keychains, enclosures or spare parts. Larger quantities get a lower price per piece. For very large runs we'll tell you honestly if injection moulding would be cheaper.

Do you sign an NDA for product designs?

Yes, happily. Your files and ideas stay private either way; we never share or reuse them. See our Terms for details.

💰 Price aur payment

How is the price calculated?

Mainly by the weight of plastic used, the material and how long it takes to print:

  • PLA: ₹5 per gram
  • PETG / ABS: ₹6 per gram
  • TPU (flexible): ₹8 per gram

Infill (how solid the inside is), quantity, colour and any design or finishing work also affect it. The instant calculator uses the same formula.

Why is the website estimate a range?

Because the exact weight depends on how we orient and support your part. The final price is confirmed after we check your file, and it's usually within the range shown. We never start before you agree to the price.

How do I pay?

UPI, bank transfer or cash for local pickup. We take 50% in advance to start, and the rest on delivery (or before dispatch for courier orders). Bigger business orders can have a different plan agreed in writing.

Do you give GST invoices?

We give an invoice for every order. Quoted prices don't include GST, shipping or extra services unless we say so, and applicable GST is charged as per Indian tax law. Share your GST details before ordering if you need them on the invoice.

Are there student discounts?

Yes. Show us your college ID and we'll give student rates on projects. Tell us your submission date early so we can plan the print.

📐 Files aur design

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, 3MF, OBJ and STEP. If you have something else (like a Fusion 360, SolidWorks or Tinkercad file), send it and we'll check. Our instant calculator reads STL files.

What's the biggest thing you can print?

About 25 × 25 × 25 cm in one piece. Bigger objects can be split into parts and joined with pins, glue or screws. We'll design the joints so they're strong and neat.

My file has errors. Can you fix it?

Yes. We check every file for holes, flipped faces, wrong units and walls that are too thin, and fix them before printing. Simple fixes are free; bigger redesigns are charged as design time, and we tell you first.

Is my STL uploaded when I use the calculator?

No. The file is read only inside your own browser to estimate the price. It reaches us only if you send it to us on WhatsApp yourself.

🧪 Material aur quality

Which material should I choose?
  • PLA: the easy default for gifts, models and display pieces. Lots of colours.
  • PETG: tougher and more water resistant, good for brackets, clips and outdoor parts.
  • ABS: handles heat better, good for car parts and enclosures.
  • TPU: flexible like rubber, for grips, gaskets and phone cases.

Not sure? Tell us what the part will do and we'll suggest one. See the materials comparison.

How accurate and strong are the parts?

Typical accuracy is about ±0.2–0.5 mm. If two parts must fit together, send us the key measurements and we'll design the clearance. Strength depends on material, infill and print direction; a mechanical engineer checks every functional part before it prints.

Will I see layer lines?

Yes, 3D printed parts have fine layer lines; that's normal for this process. We remove supports and clean every part. If you need a smoother finish, ask about sanding or a finer layer height (it takes longer).

Can you print food-safe or medical parts?

We don't recommend 3D printed parts for food contact or medical use, because tiny gaps between layers are hard to clean. Tell us your use case and we'll be honest about whether it's safe.

🚚 Delivery aur returns

How long does it take?

Most small prints are ready in 2–4 days after your advance. Design projects and large quantities take longer; we give you a date with the quote. In a hurry? Ask us; we'll tell you honestly if we can make it.

Do you deliver?

Yes: local pickup or delivery in Ahmedabad, delivery across Gujarat, and courier across India. Courier charges depend on size, weight and distance, and we show them before you pay.

What if something is wrong with my part?

Message us with your order details and photos as soon as possible, ideally within 7 days of delivery. If it doesn't match what we agreed (size, material or design) or it arrives broken, we reprint it free. If we can't fix it, we refund that part. Full details are in sections 11 and 18 of our Terms.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes. Ask us in writing (WhatsApp or email) before production begins and we'll cancel it. Once work has started, any deduction is limited to the reasonable cost of work already done and materials used; the rest is refunded. See section 17 of our Terms.
