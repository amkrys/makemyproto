2. Using this website

The instant price calculator, price examples and printer playground are for guidance only. Your STL file is read inside your own browser to estimate a price; it is not uploaded to us unless you send it to us yourself.

3. Quotes and prices

Website estimates are approximate. Your final price is the one we confirm on WhatsApp or email after checking your file or idea, and it depends on weight, material, infill, size, quantity, colour availability and any design or finishing work.

Our minimum order is ₹300. 3D design and CAD work is charged separately (from ₹300 per hour) and we tell you the estimate before starting.

A confirmed quote is valid for 7 days. Prices include our work only; delivery or courier charges are shown separately.

4. Placing an order and payment

An order is confirmed when you accept our quote and pay the advance.

  • We ask for 50% in advance before we start design or printing, and the remaining amount on delivery or before dispatch for courier orders.
  • We accept UPI, bank transfer and cash (for local pickup).
  • For large orders, business orders or long design projects, we may agree a different payment plan with you in writing.

5. Your files, designs and ideas

You keep ownership of the files, sketches, photos and ideas you send us. You give us permission to use them only to quote, design, print and deliver your order.

You confirm that you have the right to use and reproduce what you send, and that printing it doesn't infringe anyone else's copyright, trademark, design or patent.

We keep your files private and don't share them, except with you. We're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) for product designs.

If we create a new 3D design for you and you've paid for it in full, the design is yours. We may keep a copy so we can reprint it for you later; we won't sell it or share it.

We will only show photos of your part on our website or social media if you say yes first.

6. Things we won't print

We can refuse or cancel any order, and refund what you've paid, if we believe it:

  • is a weapon, a weapon part or anything designed to cause harm;
  • infringes someone else's intellectual property, or copies a branded product to pass it off as genuine;
  • is illegal, unsafe for its intended use, or offensive;
  • is meant for safety-critical, medical, food-contact or load-bearing use where a failure could hurt someone, unless we've agreed in writing on how it will be used and tested.

7. Quality and tolerances

3D printed parts are made layer by layer, so small layer lines, minor colour differences and support marks are normal. We clean every part and check it before it leaves.

Typical size accuracy is about ±0.2–0.5 mm, depending on size, material and geometry. If fit matters (for example a part that clips onto something), tell us the important measurements so we can design and check for them.

Material strength, heat resistance and flexibility vary (PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU behave differently). We'll suggest the right material, but you are responsible for checking a part is suitable for how you use it.

8. Timelines, pickup and delivery

Most small prints are ready in 2–4 days after your advance; design projects and large quantities take longer. We'll tell you the expected date with your quote. Dates are estimates, not guarantees.

You can pick up locally in Ahmedabad, get local delivery, or receive your order by courier across India. Once a parcel is handed to the courier, delivery times depend on the courier.

If a parcel arrives damaged, please take photos and message us within 48 hours of delivery so we can help.

9. Changes, cancellations and refunds

Every order is custom-made, so these rules keep things fair for both sides:

  • Before work starts: you can cancel and get your advance back in full.
  • After design or printing has started: we keep the cost of work already done (design time and material used) and refund the rest of your advance.
  • Our mistake: if a part doesn't match the confirmed design, size or material, or arrives broken, we'll reprint it free. If we can't fix it, we'll refund what you paid for that part.
  • Not covered: changes of mind after printing, designs or measurements you supplied that turn out to be wrong, and normal 3D print characteristics described in section 7.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (or UPI) within 7 working days of being approved.

If you change the design, size, material or quantity after confirming, we'll send an updated quote and timeline.
